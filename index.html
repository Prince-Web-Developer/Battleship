Battleship

Battleship is a classic two‑player strategy game where each player secretly arranges a fleet of ships on a grid. Players then take turns calling out coordinates to "fire" shots, trying to locate and sink the opponent’s ships.

  • 🎯 Objective: Sink all of your opponent’s ships before they sink yours.
  • 🛳️ Gameplay: Each ship occupies consecutive squares on the grid. Players mark hits and misses as they guess coordinates.
  • ⚔️ Winning: A ship is sunk when all its squares are hit. The first player to sink the entire fleet wins.
